Deepfake Paradigms

2 manipulation types
Paradigm 1 — Emotion Tampering

Same actor, swapped emotion audio

Video and audio come from the same actor performing the same sentence, but the audio track is replaced with a different emotional take. Voice identity is preserved — only the emotion content mismatches.

Example: actor's face shows a Neutral expression while the audio carries a Fearful delivery of the exact same sentence. Subtle — same speaker timbre, different emotional intonation.

Paradigm 2 — Cross-Identity Splicing

Video from actor A, audio from actor B

Both tracks are real, unmodified CREMA-D recordings — but spliced from two different people. This simulates face-swap or voice-cloning attacks where neither modality contains synthesis artifacts.

Example: actor 1001's face (Disgust, Low intensity) paired with actor 1065's voice (Sadness, High intensity) — both performing the same sentence. The model must detect cross-identity embedding inconsistency.

Why These Datasets?

Controlled Ground Truth

  • Scripted sentences → consistent semantic content across actors
  • Multiple intensity levels → diverse emotional gradient signal
  • Actor-level splits prevent speaker memorization
  • Lab-recorded audio → reliable mel-spectrogram extraction

Realistic Attack Simulation

  • P1 simulates dubbing attacks (emotion mismatch, same speaker)
  • P2 simulates face-swap / voice-cloning (identity mismatch)
  • Both keep individual modalities authentic — no synthesis artifacts
  • Model must detect semantic inconsistency, not compression noise

Balanced Training Conditions

  • Stage-2 uses strict 1:1 genuine:fake ratio (7,428 pairs each)
  • Stratified 50/50 P1 + P2 among fakes — no paradigm bias
  • Train / val / test splits are actor-disjoint and shared with Stage-1

Model Scope & Limitations

  • Calibrated for CREMA-D test-split clips only
  • Out-of-distribution videos (MELD, wild footage) yield unreliable scores
  • Stage-1 ~50% emotion accuracy is expected — embeddings, not predictions, matter
  • Stage-2 is trained on CREMA-D fakes; other deepfake types are out-of-distribution
